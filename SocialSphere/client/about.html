Why SocialSphere exists

Most small teams manage posts across notes apps, spreadsheets, and whatever platform they're posting to directly — which makes it hard to answer a simple question: what's going out this week, and how did last week's posts do? SocialSphere is a small, focused tool that answers that, and nothing more.

One queue

Drafts, scheduled posts, and published posts live in the same list, sorted by status so you always know what's next.

Real accounts

Every account is backed by a real database record with a securely hashed password — not a browser trick that resets when you clear cookies.

Built to grow

The backend is a plain REST API, so it's straightforward to add real publishing integrations, teammates, or approval flows later.
